Infinite Balls

A timed window where the cannon fires with no ammo cost and no cooldown. I built the whole sequence: the banner with its countdown bar, the cannon turning gold, rapid fire, and the timer meter on the button.

Wider Hole

Super Shooter

The full hero sequence: a crouch, a stretched jump, a turn and a landing shockwave, then the whirlpool that pulls in every pixel of the picked colour. A gold barrel glow and a flying cape, and he winks before he vanishes.

Pinata Bat

The bat flies in from the camera, winds up and swings right-handed into the pinata. A lattice stretches it with swing speed and whips it on impact, and the hit-stop, shake and screen punch land after the freeze.

UFO

Pick mode: the scene dims, the closed shooters open for a look, and a prompt bubble pops in. The UFO then carries the picked shooter to its slot.

Unlock reveal

Shared by all three. The tile pops in over turning rays, flies a sparkle arc to its button, and lands with a punch, an edge flash, a ring and a burst.
