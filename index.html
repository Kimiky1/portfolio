About

I’m Kim, a Unity technical artist. I sit between art and code: I build the effect, the shader or the UI flow, then make it run smoothly on a cheap phone.

I taught myself C# and Unity in 2022, and since then I’ve shipped 23 games with WizPlay, Crazy Labs, Voodoo, Clover Bite and Hoppa Play, released my own game on Steam, and now art direct Sigilbound. Claude and Tripo 3D are part of my daily pipeline, so a feature gets from idea to playable fast and the time saved goes into polish. I care most about the few seconds a player remembers: the hit, the reward, the level clear.

Super Shooter

The full hero sequence: a crouch, a stretched jump, a turn and a landing shockwave, then the whirlpool that pulls in every pixel of the picked colour. A gold barrel glow and a flying cape, and he winks before he vanishes.

Pinata Bat

The bat flies in from the camera, winds up and swings into the pinata with the right hand. A lattice stretches it with swing speed and whips it on impact, and the hit stop, shake and screen punch land after the freeze.

UFO

Unlock reveal
